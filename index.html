    Iran leans into its Hormuz campaign as transits rebound to 132 a week, and its second drone-seizure claim shifts again›

    Al Jazeera reports vessel transits through Hormuz rose to 132 in the week of Sept 21-27, up from 116 the week before — a rebound Tehran is using to argue its leverage isn't fading, not as a step toward reopening. Parliament speaker Ghalibaf said Sunday Hormuz stays shut until Tehran's conditions are met. The IRGC's account of its second drone seizure this month has already shifted, from a Remus 600 to a different vehicle, while CENTCOM insists it retains 'positive control' of its drones. Brent settled Monday at $106.89, up 2.46%.
