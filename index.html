    Strait of Hormuz shipping moves only under naval convoy as transit volumes stay near the lowest points of the conflict

    Preliminary tracking shows 52 vessel transits through the Strait of Hormuz during the week of July 27 to August 2, up from 28 the previous week, but still far below the pre-conflict range of roughly 73 to 138 transits a day; on August 2 alone, as few as two vessels transited. The safe-passage memorandum of understanding governing transit through the strait collapsed last month, and commercial vessels are now moving only under naval escort convoy. Approximately 65 ships that entered Gulf waters during a prior period of relative calm have yet to be able to leave the region. War-risk insurance for tankers transiting the strait remains at 3% to 10% of a vessel's hull value, versus about 0.25% before the conflict began.
