    Iran's UN delegation left New York Monday with no deal; Trump says “we blow them up or make a deal”›

    Iran's UN delegation, led by Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, left New York on Monday evening for Doha. Iran's UN mission says the departure followed a schedule it gave the US State Department on Sept 17. On Wednesday, two unnamed US officials told Axios and the Associated Press that Secretary of State Marco Rubio had ordered the delegation out after talks via Qatari mediators stalled; the State Department has said nothing on record, and Iran's mission calls the account “baseless.” Trump said Wednesday he must decide whether to “blow them up or make a deal.” ICE Brent closed down 2.4% at $97.46 on Sept 30.

    Why it mattersBoth sides agree only on the Monday departure; the expulsion account rests on anonymous US officials, and oil's fall shows traders are not betting on a military move.
    What to doTreat the departure as fact and the expulsion as an unconfirmed US claim; watch Iran's next statement from Doha and Brent's next close.
