    Talks collapse: Rubio orders Iran's delegation out of New York as Trump says “we blow them up or make a deal”›

    Secretary of State Marco Rubio ordered Iran's UN delegation, led by Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, to leave New York on Monday night after a week of diplomacy collapsed into stalemate, Axios first reported Wednesday; the delegation flew to Doha at 1:20am Tuesday. President Trump told reporters at the White House on Wednesday he must now decide whether to “blow them up or make a deal,” adding the war “is going to end very soon, one way or another.” The rupture follows Trump's Sept 26 rejection of Iran's seven-day Hormuz-reopening proposal, tied to lifting the US naval blockade, oil-sanctions relief and $12 billion in frozen assets.

    Why it mattersA diplomatic expulsion is a harder signal than a rejected proposal — it suggests Washington sees nothing left to extract from this round of talks, raising the odds the next move is military rather than diplomatic.
    What to doWatch for Iran's own next statement out of Doha rather than reading the expulsion alone — it raises the near-term odds of the escalation Trump has openly flagged, but doesn't foreclose a later resumption.
