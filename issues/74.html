    Iran's parliament speaker vows Hormuz stays shut as IRGC claims a second US drone seizure and Trump still expects talks this week›

    Iran's parliament speaker Ghalibaf said Sunday Hormuz stays shut until Tehran's conditions are met, hardening the freeze since Trump's Saturday rejection of Iran's seven-day reopening plan. Hours later the IRGC said it seized a second US underwater drone this month, a Remus 600, while US Central Command called the claim 'clearly desperate,' saying it retains 'positive control' of all its drones. Trump told Axios he still expects talks with Iran this week, with Qatar-mediated talks possibly resuming Monday.

    Why it mattersa hardened public line paired with an unverifiable drone dispute shows both governments performing conflict for domestic audiences even as quiet diplomacy reportedly continues.
